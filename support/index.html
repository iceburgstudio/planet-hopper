Planet Hopper Support

How to reach a human, and answers to the handful of things people actually write in about.

Updated 1 September 2026 Applies to Planet Hopper for iOS com.theplanethopper.game

The short version

Email iceburgstudio.dev@gmail.com. One person reads that inbox and answers every message, usually within a couple of days.

Get in touch

There is no support ticket system and no bot — just the email address above. You will get a faster answer if you include:

  • Your device and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • For anything involving a purchase, the order ID from your App Store receipt. We cannot look up your account — we have no accounts — so that receipt is the only thing that identifies the transaction.

Purchases

I bought a coin pack and the coins never arrived

Coins are added the moment Apple confirms the payment, so this is nearly always a transaction still in flight rather than one that failed. Open the game and let it sit on the main menu for a few seconds while connected to the internet — the game keeps listening for completed purchases from the moment it launches, including ones approved later, such as an Ask to Buy request a parent has to approve. If the coins still are not there, email us with the order ID and we will sort it out.

Can I restore my coin packs on a new device?

No, and this is not a bug. Coin packs are consumable purchases — you buy coins, you spend them on characters and cosmetics, and they are gone. Apple does not restore consumables the way it restores an unlocked app feature. Anything you already bought with those coins stays unlocked on the device that earned it.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never see your payment details and cannot issue one. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com. If the refund is because something in the game went wrong, write to us as well so we can fix the underlying problem.

Progress and saves

I got a new phone and my progress is gone

Your progress — planets cleared, coins, characters, cosmetics — is stored on the device only. There is no account, no cloud save, and no copy of it on any server of ours, which also means there is nothing for us to restore. Deleting the app deletes the save with it. We would rather tell you that plainly than have you email us hoping we can recover it.

Unlocking things

Most of the planets are locked

They unlock in order. Earth is open from the start, and each later planet needs the previous planet’s boss beaten. That is the campaign, not a paywall — no purchase unlocks a planet.

Infinite Mode is greyed out

Infinite Mode opens once you have beaten one boss. It then remixes only the worlds you have actually cleared, so it keeps growing as you do.

The leaderboard is empty

The Game Center leaderboard ranks Infinite Mode scores, so it has nothing to show until Infinite Mode is unlocked and you have posted a run.

Do I have to sign in to Game Center?

No. Declining is fully supported — the whole game is playable signed out, and your best score is kept locally either way. You will only miss the global board and achievements.

Ads

There are exactly two ads in Planet Hopper and both are optional. Nothing interrupts you: there are no interstitials between runs and no banners anywhere.

  • A coin boost offer on the main menu, which doubles your coins for a while if you choose to watch. It only appears after you have played a number of runs, and saying no makes it go quiet for a good while.
  • A second chance on the Adventure death screen, offering to continue the run you just lost. Once per run, and only if you tap it.

If iOS asked whether to allow tracking and you said no, that is respected and the game plays exactly the same. What the ad provider collects either way is set out in the privacy policy.

Found a bug, or want something changed?

Send it to the same address. Bug reports that name the planet and roughly what was on screen are the ones that get fixed fastest. Suggestions are read too, even when the answer has to be no.
